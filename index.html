ABOUT

Aoxin Reivox is a DJ and music producer from Kerala, India.

His musical journey began at 17 as a bedroom producer, exploring electronic music, energetic rhythms, emotional melodies and different sounds while developing his own identity.

His music includes house, EDM, progressive house, big room, future bass, baile and experimental edits, with a strong connection to energetic and emotional sounds.

2026 marks a new chapter as Aoxin Reivox begins his journey as a DJ and starts performing live gigs.
